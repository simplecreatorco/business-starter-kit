Picking a Name (Without Overthinking It)

People get stuck on their brand name for months. Here's the truth: your name matters less than you think. What matters is that you pick one and move forward.

A few tips:

  • Keep it simple and easy to spell
  • Make sure it's not already taken (search Instagram, Google, and your state's business registry)
  • It should give people a hint of what you do or how you make them feel
  • Say it out loud 10 times. Does it feel good?

Sole Proprietor vs. LLC — The Quick Version

You do not need a law degree for this. Here's the simple breakdown:

Sole Proprietor: The easiest option. You are the business. No paperwork to file (in most states). You can start selling today. The downside? Your personal assets aren't protected if something goes wrong.

LLC: Separates your personal stuff from your business stuff. Costs $50-500 depending on your state. Takes 15-30 minutes to set up online. Recommended once you start making real money.

💡 Rebecca's take: Start as a sole proprietor if you're just getting going. Once you're consistently making money, file for an LLC. Don't let this step keep you from starting.

Getting Your EIN

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is like a social security number for your business. It's free and takes 5 minutes on the IRS website. You'll need it for taxes and to open a business bank account.

Why a Custom Email Matters

A custom email instantly signals "I'm a real business." It's also necessary for email marketing later (services like Flodesk need a domain-based email to send from).

The most popular option is Google Workspace — it's $6/month and gives you Gmail with your own domain. If you're on a tighter budget, your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap) usually includes basic email for free or cheap.

✓
Choose your email format: hello@, info@, or yourname@
✓
Sign up for Google Workspace (or use your registrar's email)
✓
Verify your domain (follow the setup wizard)
✓
Send yourself a test email from your new address
✓
Update your Instagram bio and link-in-bio with your new email
💡 Don't overcomplicate this. You need one email address right now. You can always add more later (support@, team@, whatever). Just get one set up and move on.

The Simple Bookkeeping System

Bookkeeping sounds intimidating, but all it really means is: know what money comes in, know what money goes out, and keep it separate from your personal spending.

That's it. You do not need QuickBooks right now. You need a Google Sheet.

What to Track

  • Income: Every sale, broken down by product
  • Expenses: Software subscriptions, ad spend, templates you buy, courses
  • Tax set-aside: Set aside 25-30% of profit for taxes (open a separate savings account for this)

Do this once a week. Pick a day — maybe Friday afternoon — and spend 10 minutes updating your sheet. Future you will be so grateful.

Do You Even Need a Website Right Now?

If you're selling digital products through Flodesk Checkout or a platform like Stan Store, you can technically start without a website. But having one — even a simple one — makes everything feel more legitimate.

The good news: you don't need 47 pages. You need 3-4:

  • Homepage: Who you are, what you do, one clear CTA
  • Sales page: For your main product
  • About page: Your story (people buy from people)
  • Contact page: How to reach you

Platform Options

Showit (recommended): Drag-and-drop, gorgeous templates, designer-friendly. They have free templates to start with.

Flodesk Checkout: If you're already using Flodesk for email, their checkout pages can serve as a mini sales page while you build a proper site.

Stan Store / Link-in-bio tools: A good temporary solution. Not a replacement for a real website long-term, but it works to start.

Why Your Space Matters

When you have a designated workspace — even if it's tiny — your brain switches into "work mode" when you sit down. It's a physical signal that this is real.

You don't need a home office. You need a spot that's yours. A fold-down desk in the corner. A lap desk on the couch. A specific chair at the kitchen table. Whatever works.

Setting Up Your Space

A few things that make a big difference:

  • Good lighting (even a cheap desk lamp helps)
  • A notebook or planner for quick brain dumps
  • Headphones (especially if you're working during nap time)
  • A water bottle (stay hydrated, CEO)
  • Something that makes you smile — a candle, a plant, a cute mug

Set Your Boundaries

Written Lesson

Choose Your Work Hours

You started this business for freedom. Don't accidentally build yourself a 24/7 job.

Pick your work hours and write them down. If nap time is your window, great — that's 1-2 hours a day. If it's 8-10 PM after bedtime, that works too. But at your chosen end time, close the laptop. For real.

There will always be one more thing to do. That one more thing will be there tomorrow.

Turn Off Notifications

This one is non-negotiable. Right now, go to your phone settings and turn off Instagram notifications. All of them. Push notifications, DM notifications, like notifications. All of it.

Nothing on Instagram is an emergency. Nothing in your inbox is an emergency. You will check these things during your work hours, and that is enough.

Responding to Emails and DMs

When you're new, every DM feels like it needs an immediate response. It doesn't. Here's your new rule:

Check and respond to DMs and emails once or twice a day, during your work hours. That's it. Nobody is going to stop buying from you because you took 4 hours to reply.

Talk to Your People

If you have a partner, spouse, or family members in your house, have the conversation. Tell them when your work hours are. Tell them what you need during that time. This isn't selfish — it's how businesses survive.

Even a simple "Hey, I'm working from 1-3 during nap time. Can you handle things if one of the kids wakes up?" goes a long way.

✓
Write down your work hours (post them somewhere visible)
✓
Turn off Instagram notifications right now
✓
Set specific times to check email and DMs (2x per day max)
✓
Have the conversation with your partner/family about your work time
✓
Delete or mute any group chats that drain your energy
💡 Remember: You are building this business to support your life, not take it over. Boundaries aren't a luxury — they're the foundation of a business that lasts.
